  • Typography that fits — a demo of three skills
  • Two typefaces on one baseline
    The same sentence twice, overlaid. The reference face stays put, in grey. The other face is the one you adjust, in red; where they overlap it goes dark. Drag its size until the x-heights meet, its weight until the stems do, then its letter-spacing until the gaps between letters do. The snap points are the skills' numbers.
  • The EDS type scale, step by step
    One base, one ratio, one snap for the sizes. One curve, one grid for the line-heights. Move the step and watch the arithmetic.
